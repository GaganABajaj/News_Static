Indian Markets Hold Steady as Oil Prices and Global Yields Weigh, IT Sector Leads Pull‑back

After the sharpest 10‑week drop on Thursday, India’s Nifty\u202f50 and Sensex edged higher on Friday, buoyed by small‑cap gains while the IT sector slipped on high crude prices and rising U.S. Treasury yields. The market outlook remains cautious amid inflation fears, elevated oil, and geopolitical uncertainty.

By GaganPublished September 25, 2026 · 5:50 PM
FIELD NOTE / DAILY EDITION

### What Happened? \n- **Market movement:** The Nifty\u202f50 rose **0.09%** to **23,084.4** and the BSE Sensex climbed **0.13%** to **73,674.57** as of 9:39\u202fa.m. IST on\u202fFriday, September\u202f25,\u202f2026. \n- **Sector performance:** Ten of the 16 major sectors posted gains, offsetting a broad sell‑off the day before. The IT index fell **0.7%**, while small‑caps rose **0.2%** and mid‑caps were flat. \n- **Stock highlights:** \n * **Meesho** dropped ~4% after Nomura initiated coverage with a “reduce” rating and forecast a 28% downside. \n * **Welspun Corp** jumped 4% after securing a $412. 5\u202fmillion order for high‑frequency‑induction‑welded pipes. \n * **Syrma SGS Technology** rose 2.1% after Axis Capital upgraded its recommendation to “buy” and lifted the target price to ₹1,956. \n * **Vascon Engineers** surged 10% on a $69\u202fmillion contract win. \n\n### Where? \n- The activity unfolded on India’s two primary equity venues: the **National Stock Exchange (NSE)** and the **Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE)** in **Mumbai**. \n\n### When? \n- Trading data are from the **morning session of Friday, 25\u202fSeptember\u202f2026** (9:39\u202fa.m. IST). The article was posted at **07:48\u202fa.m. GMT+5:30** and updated an hour later. \n\n### Why? \n- **High crude oil prices** (above $100\u202fper barrel) revived inflation concerns and stoked expectations of additional **Federal Reserve rate hikes**, dampening sentiment in export‑oriented sectors such as IT. \n- **Rising global bond yields** added pressure on risk assets, while the unresolved **U.S.–Iran tension** contributed to market uncertainty. \n- The previous day’s steep declines were also driven by **proposed insurance‑commission caps** that hurt financial‑sector stocks.
