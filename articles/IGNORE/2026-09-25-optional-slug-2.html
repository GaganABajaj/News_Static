What Happened?

Market movement: The Nifty 50 rose 0.09% to 23,084.4 and the BSE Sensex climbed 0.13% to 73,674.57 as of 9:39 a.m. IST on Friday, September 25, 2026.

  • Sector performance: Ten of the 16 major sectors posted gains, offsetting a broad sell‑off the day before. The IT index fell 0.7%, while small‑caps rose 0.2% and mid‑caps were flat.
  • Stock highlights:
  • Meesho dropped ~4% after Nomura initiated coverage with a “reduce” rating and forecast a 28% downside.
  • Welspun Corp jumped 4% after securing a $412.5 million order for high‑frequency‑induction‑welded pipes.
  • Syrma SGS Technology rose 2.1% after Axis Capital upgraded its recommendation to “buy” and lifted the target price to ₹1,956.
  • Vascon Engineers surged 10% on a $69 million contract win.

Where?

  • The activity unfolded on India’s two primary equity venues: the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) in Mumbai.

When?

  • Trading data are from the morning session of Friday, 25 September 2026 (9:39 a.m. IST). The article was posted at 07:48 a.m. GMT+5:30 and updated an hour later.

Why?

  • High crude oil prices (above $100 per barrel) revived inflation concerns and stoked expectations of additional Federal Reserve rate hikes, dampening sentiment in export‑oriented sectors such as IT.
  • Rising global bond yields added pressure on risk assets, while the unresolved U.S.–Iran tension contributed to market uncertainty.
  • The previous day’s steep declines were also driven by proposed insurance‑commission caps that hurt financial‑sector stocks.
