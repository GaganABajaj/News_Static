Red & Orange Weather Alerts Sweep Uttarakhand – Heavy Rains, Landslides, and Road Blockages Until Sept 28, 2026

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued red and orange alerts for multiple districts of Uttarakhand as an intense system of rain, thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds sweeps the state from the night of Sep 25 through Sep 28 2026, with snowfall expected above 4,000 m. Authorities warn of landslides, flash‑floods, road closures and disruptions to transport and essential services, urging pilgrims, tourists and commuters to avoid non‑essential travel.

What happened?

  • The IMD placed several districts of Uttarakhand under red (highest) and orange (high) weather alerts.
  • An intense spell of heavy rain, thunderstorms, lightning, gusty winds (30‑40 km/h) and snowfall at elevations above 4,000 m is forecast.
  • More than 33 roads are blocked across the state (14 in Chamoli, 10 in Dehradun); restoration work is under way.
  • Authorities have cautioned against non‑essential travel, warned of landslides, rock‑falls, mud‑slides, flash‑floods, and possible disruptions to electricity, water, rail, air and helicopter services.

Where?

  • Affected districts (red alert on Sep 26): Bageshwar, Nainital, Pithoragarh, Champawat, Udham Singh Nagar.
  • Orange‑alert districts (Sep 26): Dehradun, Pauri Garhwal, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Almora.
  • Additional red alerts on Sep 27: Tehri Garhwal, Haridwar, Pauri Garhwal; orange alerts on Sep 27: Nainital, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Uttarkashi.
  • Specific incidents reported in Pithoragarh where stones and debris fell onto NH‑9, causing long queues and stranding patients and a funeral procession.

When?

  • Alert period: Friday night (Sept 25) through the forenoon of Monday (Sept 28), 2026.
  • Rain intensity peaks on Sept 26‑27 with light‑to‑moderate rain and thunderstorms; light‑to‑moderate rain continues on Sept 28.

Why?

  • The weather pattern results from the combined impact of two weather systems that are driving the prolonged rainfall and associated hazards.

Impact & Advisory Highlights

  • Pilgrims & tourists on Char Dham routes and other hill destinations are urged to stay alert and avoid travel.
  • Helicopter flights to Kedarnath Dham may be halted if weather worsens.
  • Schools, construction, and mountaineering expeditions are advised to be suspended.
  • District tourism officer (Rudraprayag) and disaster officials emphasize caution on blocked or water‑logged roads.
