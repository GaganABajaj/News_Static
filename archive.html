SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY Friday

India Launches Bio‑Based PLA Biodegradable Plastic Pilot under BioE3, Backed by ₹75 Cr Grant

Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh formally inaugurated a ₹75 crore, pilot‑scale facility to produce polylactic‑acid (PLA) biodegradable plastic at Balrampur Chini Mills (BCML) in Kumbhi, Uttar Pradesh. Funded through the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) under the government’s BioE3 framework, the 100 tonnes‑per‑annum (TPA) plant will develop advanced PLA grades and co‑polymers, turning sugar‑derived feedstock into high‑value, eco‑friendly materials and showcasing India’s “waste‑to‑wealth” bio‑economy strategy.
